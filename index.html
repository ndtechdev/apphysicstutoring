Our Vision

AP Physics can feel overwhelming because it combines conceptual science, algebra, trigonometry, and problem solving. Our goal is to help students build a strong foundation early so they feel prepared, confident, and ready to succeed.

Semester Plan

Topics covered

The class focuses on the prerequisite math and physics concepts students need before AP Physics.

∑

Algebra for Physics

Review equations, variables, units, graph reading, and solving for unknowns.

△

Trigonometry Basics

Learn sine, cosine, tangent, vectors, and components used in physics problems.

→

Motion

Understand position, velocity, acceleration, graphs, and kinematics equations.

F

Forces

Learn Newton’s Laws, free-body diagrams, friction, tension, and net force.

E

Energy & Momentum

Cover work, energy, conservation, impulse, and momentum-based problems.

↻

Rotation & Waves

Introduce circular motion, torque, rotational concepts, and wave basics.
